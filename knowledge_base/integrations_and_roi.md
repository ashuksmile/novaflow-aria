# NovaFlow AI — Integrations & Customer ROI Case Studies

## Native Integrations
- **CRM:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive, Freshsales, and custom REST Webhooks.
- **Calendar:** Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook / Office 365, Calendly.
- **Telephony & Voice:** Twilio, Exotel, Plivo, Vonage, and WebRTC browser widgets.
- **Collaboration:** Slack, Microsoft Teams (instant alerts when a high-intent lead books a demo).

## Verified Customer ROI Case Studies
1. **FinEdge Technologies (B2B Fintech, 40 reps):**
   - Reduced average lead response time from 4 hours to 8 seconds.
   - Increased qualified demo bookings by **3.2x** within 60 days.
   - Saved SDRs 14 hours per week in manual CRM updates.
2. **CloudScale Systems (Enterprise SaaS, 120 reps):**
   - Deployed bilingual (English + Hindi) inbound voice qualification across India and APAC.
   - Cut Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL) by **58%** compared to scaling an outbound call center.
   - Achieved full payback period in **5 weeks**.
3. **BrightPath Logistics (B2B Supply Chain):**
   - Automated 4,200 monthly qualification calls with a 91% caller satisfaction score.
