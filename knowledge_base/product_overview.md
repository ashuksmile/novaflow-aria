# NovaFlow AI — Product Overview

## What is NovaFlow AI?
NovaFlow AI is a B2B Revenue Operations (RevOps) and AI Sales Automation platform built for high-growth SaaS and enterprise sales teams. It automates outbound qualification, inbound lead response, CRM hygiene, and pipeline forecasting.

## Core Capabilities
1. **AI Voice & Chat Qualification:** Responds to inbound leads in under 5 seconds across English and Hindi, qualifying prospects using BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline).
2. **Automated CRM Sync:** Logs call transcripts, sentiment scores, objections, and next steps directly into Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, or Pipedrive with zero manual rep data entry.
3. **Smart Meeting Scheduler:** Checks sales rep calendars in real time and books qualified discovery calls directly during the conversation.
4. **Revenue Intelligence Dashboard:** Tracks conversion rates, common buyer objections, call sentiment trends, and pipeline velocity.

## Target Customers
- Mid-market and enterprise B2B teams (10 to 500+ sales reps)
- SaaS companies, IT services, fintech, and B2B marketplaces handling 500+ inbound or outbound leads per month.
- Teams struggling with slow lead response times (speed-to-lead), high SDR turnover, or messy CRM records.

## Deployment & Onboarding Time
- Standard cloud setup takes less than 48 hours.
- Native OAuth connectors for CRM and calendar take 15 minutes to configure.
- Dedicated solutions engineer included on Growth and Enterprise plans.
