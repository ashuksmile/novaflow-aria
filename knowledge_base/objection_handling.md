# NovaFlow AI — Common Objections & Sales Playbook

## Objection: "It is too expensive / We don't have budget right now"
- Compare against hiring even one full-time SDR ($4,000–$6,000/month in the US or INR 50,000–80,000/month in India).
- NovaFlow Starter ($299/mo) or Growth ($799/mo) runs 24/7 with zero ramp time and typically pays for itself within 4 to 6 weeks via higher demo conversion.
- Offer the 14-day guided pilot so they can verify ROI before committing to an annual contract.

## Objection: "We already use HubSpot / Salesforce"
- NovaFlow does not replace HubSpot or Salesforce — it supercharges them.
- It sits in front of the CRM, conducts live voice qualification, and automatically writes clean BANT fields, sentiment scores, and call summaries into their existing CRM.

## Objection: "Will our prospects be annoyed talking to an AI?"
- NovaFlow responds in under 1.5 seconds with natural conversational voices in English and Hindi, handles interruptions gracefully, and immediately transfers or books a human specialist when requested.
- Customers report 90%+ caller satisfaction because prospects get instant answers instead of waiting hours for a callback.

## Objection: "We need IT/Security approval first"
- Highlight SOC 2 Type II, GDPR & India DPDP compliance, AES-256 encryption, PII redaction, and zero model training on customer data.
- Offer to send our Security Whitepaper during a 20-minute technical demo with their team.
