# NovaFlow AI — Security, Privacy & Compliance

## Certifications & Standards
- **SOC 2 Type II** certified (annual independent audit).
- **ISO 27001** aligned security management framework.
- **GDPR** and **India DPDP Act 2023** compliant data handling with configurable regional data residency (US-East, EU-Frankfurt, India-Mumbai).

## Data Encryption & Protection
- **In Transit:** TLS 1.3 for all web, WebSocket, and API traffic; SRTP for telephony audio streams.
- **At Rest:** AES-256 encryption for databases, vector indexes, and call recordings.
- **PII Redaction:** Automated masking of credit card numbers, SSNs, Aadhaar/PAN numbers, and sensitive personal identifiers before LLM logging or analytics storage.

## AI Model & Zero Data Retention
- Customer call transcripts and knowledge base documents are **never used to train public or shared foundation models**.
- Zero Data Retention (ZDR) agreements with enterprise LLM inference providers.
- Configurable audio recording retention (7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or immediate deletion after transcription).

## Access Control
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), SAML 2.0 / OIDC SSO (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace), and immutable audit trails.
